# The Unbound

A side-on action game. Time has broken, and Wren walks backward through it, back in time toward the rift where her sister Sol was lost, holding off the artifacts of fractured time that keep pouring out of the collapse.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's a single self-contained file with no build step and no dependencies. The only external request is a Google Fonts stylesheet.

- **Aim:** move the mouse, or drag up and down on a touchscreen. Your gun fires on its own.
- **Rewind step:** the « button on the left, or Space.
- **Consumables:** the buttons in the bottom-left, or keys 1–4.

Progress saves to the browser's local storage.

## Progression

| Currency | Earned from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| Chronons | Kills and distance reached | Gun upgrades, Sight, Salvage, consumables |
| Anchors | Clearing a rift | Weapons and kit |
| Paradox | Starting over (1 per rift cleared that cycle) | Permanent perks |
