# The Unbound — story notes

Working notes on the story and how it ties into the mechanics. Decided items are marked; everything else is open.

## Premise (decided)

Time broke. A moment cracked and everything around it fell out of order: eras slid into each other and the world came **unbound** from time.

**Sol** was at the centre of it and was lost. Her brother **Wren** goes in after her. The story the bosses slowly reveal: **Wren caused it.**

## Characters (decided)

- **Wren** — the player character. Long coat, amber visor, trailing scarf. He/him.
- **Sol** — Wren's sister, lost in the Unbound, half-unbound when he reaches her.
- **Sync** — a small floating computer companion (think Destiny's Ghost). Opens rifts, carries the anchors, and deploys Wren's gear.

## Why you walk backward (decided)

In the Unbound you travel back in time by stepping into the past. Turn your back on the collapse and it takes you, so Wren walks backward the whole way, eyes on what's coming. Every rift is one step further back toward the moment Sol was lost.

In game, distance is shown as **time walked back** (days, then years).

## Opening a rift (decided)

Rifts aren't just there at the end of a level. When Wren has gone far enough back:

1. A **golden, jagged crack in time** appears.
2. Sync flies to it and starts prying it open.
3. Wren has to **stop and hold the line** for several seconds while Sync works. The echoes keep coming.
4. The crack breaks open and Wren jumps through.

## Structure: chapters (decided)

The game is told in **chapters**. Each one is a set of **5 rifts** ending in a **boss**. There are at least 3 chapters, maybe 5.

**Chapter 1 (first cycle):**
1. Play rifts 1–5 and beat the boss.
2. Win an **anchor**.
3. Mini cutscene: Sync absorbs the anchor and opens the crack, but this time the anchor floats above Sync and the crack turns **blue**. The rift leads to **Sol**.
4. A short scene, silent-film style (implied dialogue, title cards or none). Wren takes the anchor from Sync and tries to give it to Sol. It doesn't work. The unbound part of her takes over, and **she kills him**. This sends Wren back to the start.

**Every chapter after that:**
1. Reach rift 5 again and beat the boss. Sync offers the anchor.
2. **Wren shakes his head.** Sync puts it away and rips open a normal rift instead.
3. That rift leads to a **new biome** and **5 new rifts**, ending in a new boss and a new anchor.
4. Take that anchor to Sol. Each time, the scene with her gets a little further.

**Ending:** After enough anchors, Wren finally brings Sol all the way back. The game ends there, implying the two of them now set out to fix the whole world.

## Enemies (decided)

Echoes and everything else that attacks you are **artifacts of fractured time**, not people. This leaves room for any kind of creature.

- **Echoes** (drifters, splitters, hulks, phasers, mites) — walk at you along the ground.
- **Wraiths** — come out of tears in the sky and dive.

## Bosses (direction decided)

There's no full dodge-based combat, so bosses are built around what the player can already do:

1. **Pursuers** — you don't have to kill them, just reach the rift first. Hitting weak points knocks them back.
2. **Shoot-down attacks** — they throw things you have to shoot out of the air.

The rewind step is the one active defensive move. **Each boss tells part of the story** of how Wren caused the break.

### Chapter 1 boss: The Hollow Hour (rift 5) — built

A hunched colossus of white echo-plating that crawls after Wren on long arms. Its head is a broken clock face with a single **amber** eye: Wren's colour. Its entrance card reads *"Something in here is wearing your colour."* That's the first hint that Wren caused this.

- Crawls slightly faster than Wren walks; if it reaches him, he dies.
- Three amber cores open and close. Hitting an open core damages it and knocks it back.
- Throws dark crystal shards in arcs that have to be shot down (or jabbed by the spear).
- Enrages during the hold: faster, more shards, cores stay closed for less time.
- Killing it is optional; reaching the rift is the win. Killing it pays a burst of anomalies.
- Clearing rift 5 ends chapter 1 and Sync recovers the first **anchor**.

### Sol, first meeting — built

Plays the first time Wren finishes the hold at rift 5. Silent-film style: letterboxed, no voices, intertitle cards in a serif italic.

1. The anchor (a faceted crystal of cold blue light) rises above Sync at the crack. The crack turns blue and tears open.
2. A still place: a huge stopped clock, drifting motes, a glassy floor. Sol stands with her back turned; her back half is unbound (white echo plating, colour-fringed, flickering).
3. Wren walks in. **"Sol?"** She turns. **"Wren…? You came."**
4. The anchor floats from Sync to Wren's hand. He steps closer and holds it out. **"Take it. Come home."**
5. She reaches for it. The anchor flickers and goes dark. Her unbound half floods over her; her eye goes red. **"I can't. It won't let me go."**
6. A white tentacle-arm lashes out of her and through Wren; he shatters. **"Not yet."**
7. Fade to black → paradox is paid and the cycle resets to rift 1.

Later clears of rift 5: the anchor rises again, **Wren shakes his head**, Sync tucks it away and tears a normal gold rift. The run continues to rift 6.

## Mechanics in story terms

| Mechanic | Story meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Anomalies (was chronons) | Loose shards of time the artifacts break into | Decided |
| Sparks (was the per-rift anchor currency) | Sync's power, earned each rift; buys weapons, gear and Sync upgrades | Decided |
| Stasis cores | Frozen fragments of the original broken moment; going off shows a rewinding clock face | Decided |
| Sky tears / Wraiths | The Unbound noticing you and sending hunters | Decided |
| Rewind step (dodge) | Wren snaps a second further back in time | Decided |
| Anchors | One per chapter, won from that chapter's boss; the key to bringing Sol back | Decided |
| **Gear** tab | What Wren carries: Phase shield, Spear, Sight, Anomaly Collector, Chrono boots | Decided |
| **Sync** tab | What Sync runs: Mounted weapon, Auto-deployer, Stasis capacitor, Crit sight, Rift breaker (faster rift opening) | Decided |
| Paradox | Earned when Sol kills Wren; that death *is* the reset. No manual start-over once Sol's scene exists | Decided |

## Gear notes

- **Spear** (Wren's) — melee jab that fires automatically when something gets close. Works on charges that refill one at a time, like Zarya's bubbles in Overwatch. Upgrades add charges.
- **Holding a rift** — starts at 8 seconds; Sync's Rift breaker upgrade brings it down to 3.5.
