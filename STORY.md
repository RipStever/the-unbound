# The Unbound — story notes

Working notes on the story and how it ties into the mechanics. Decided items are marked; everything else is open.

## Premise (decided)

Time broke. A moment cracked and everything around it fell out of order: eras slid into each other and the world came **unbound** from time.

**Sol** was at the centre of it and was lost. Her sibling **Wren** goes in after her.

## Characters (decided)

- **Wren** — the player character. Long coat, amber visor, trailing scarf.
- **Sol** — Wren's sister, lost in the Unbound.

## Why you walk backward (decided)

In the Unbound you travel back in time by stepping into the past. Turn your back on the collapse and it takes you, so Wren walks backward the whole way, eyes on what's coming. Every rift is one step further back toward the moment Sol was lost.

In game, distance is shown as **time walked back** (days, then years).

## Enemies (decided)

Echoes and everything else that attacks you are **artifacts of fractured time**, not people. This leaves room for any kind of creature.

- **Echoes** (drifters, splitters, hulks, phasers, mites) — walk at you along the ground.
- **Wraiths** — come out of tears in the sky and dive.

## Mechanics in story terms

| Mechanic | Story meaning | Status |

| Chronons | Loose shards of time the artifacts break into | Decided |
| Stasis cores | Frozen fragments of the original broken moment; going off shows a rewinding clock face | Decided |

| Rewind step (dodge) | Wren snaps a second further back in time | Decided |

## Ending (direction decided, details open)

Hades-style: the full story takes many complete runs.

- When Wren finally reaches Sol, she is **half-unbound**: part herself, part artifact.
- Bringing her all the way back takes several full clears. **How** is open; likely tied to whatever anchors become.

## Bosses (direction decided)

There is no dodge-heavy combat, so bosses are built around what the player can already do:

1. **Pursuers** — you don't have to kill them, just reach the rift first. Hitting weak points knocks them back.
2. **Shoot-down attacks** — they throw things you have to shoot out of the air.

The rewind step gives one active defensive move on a cooldown.
